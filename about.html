ABOUT PBCC

Pangantucan Bukidnon Community College (PBCC) is a community college in Pangantucan, Bukidnon. The college seal is marked 2014. PBCC does not publish a separate official website. Its public page is facebook.com/pangbukcc.

This portal is the college library and attendance system. Students and staff can search the catalog, register for library access, record attendance, and request a room.
